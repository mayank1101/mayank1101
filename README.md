### About me

I've been working in AI for 4+ years, mostly in research, healthcare and manufacturing. Somewhere along the way, I got more interested in a different question than "can we build it?": **does it actually work, and is it safe to put in front of real people?** That's what I spend most of my time on now.

- 🧪 **Testing LLMs properly:** I build golden test sets with domain experts, use LLM-as-judge to catch wrong answers and hallucinations, and keep humans in the loop before anything goes live.
- 🛡️ **Trying to break things:** I like poking at AI systems with prompt injections and edge cases, and checking that guardrails, privacy and access controls hold up.
- 📚 **Research:** I co-authored **OpenMedLM (AAAI 2024)**, a study on how far open-source LLMs can go in medical question answering.
- 🏭 **Real-world GenAI:** I've worked on RAG assistants and data platforms that real teams rely on every day.
- ⚡ **Making models practical:** I've made NLP models smaller and faster so they're cheap enough to run in production.
